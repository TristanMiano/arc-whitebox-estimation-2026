## Method and contribution

Learn local mean innovations from intermediate-layer supervision and propagate a separate response vector. Small neural source and combined objective are project additions; learned closure is an established idea.

Exact T166 P45 source and JSON asset. Replace ALG051 terminal124-coefficient correction with a644-parameter nonlinear local mean source, propagated along the unchanged physical path. Same physical state, first15 outputs and inherited defaults.

All entries solve the same Phase 2 problem: predict the layerwise expected ReLU activations of a known width-1024, depth-16 random MLP under standard-normal input. The objective is the mean of per-network final-layer MSE multiplied by `max(0.1, FLOPs / 2**41)`. A lower raw error can still be a worse adjusted score if compute grows too much.
