## Method and contribution

Add a 32-field covariance-defect dictionary with 96 fitted coefficients in three depth bins. Unlike a terminal calibration, this changes the physical covariance trajectory and later means.

Exact T170 P49 source and incumbent neural asset. Add32 analytic covariance-defect fields with96 fixed coefficients in3 layer bins; marginal, mixed21/22/31 and quadratic third-cumulant operators. Hermite8 Gaussian reference. Physical covariance trajectory changes; the incumbent mean-source recipe and other defaults are retained.

All entries solve the same Phase 2 problem: predict the layerwise expected ReLU activations of a known width-1024, depth-16 random MLP under standard-normal input. The objective is the mean of per-network final-layer MSE multiplied by `max(0.1, FLOPs / 2**41)`. A lower raw error can still be a worse adjusted score if compute grows too much.
