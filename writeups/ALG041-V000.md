## Method and contribution

Adds the locally derived/implemented JSP fresh-source joint response to ALG038-V001 and recomputes repeated-entry completion. No local fit. Related cumulant-response ideas are published; this record does not establish academic priority.

H143-JSP: replace off-diagonal fresh B source by F+HG+HS+HAD, using pre-activation mup,Cp,ap,Dp,Sp and returned Gaussian d,e. Recompute repeated-index completion against changed B. Preserve all old third history, ephemeral E, radial fourth h, diagonal convention and scaling. No E1 or added fourth path.

All entries solve the same Phase 2 problem: predict the layerwise expected ReLU activations of a known width-1024, depth-16 random MLP under standard-normal input. The objective is the mean of per-network final-layer MSE multiplied by `max(0.1, FLOPs / 2**41)`. A lower raw error can still be a worse adjusted score if compute grows too much.
