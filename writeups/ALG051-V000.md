## Method and contribution

Add 124 fitted coefficients predicting propagated mean residuals from marginal and pair fields. The physical recurrence is inherited from ALG050; public-training supervision is explicit.

Exact T163 P67 source: add a frozen124-coefficient marginal/pair residual to ALG050 final mean via one propagated vector. Parent physical trajectory, first15 outputs, defaults and charged diagnostics unchanged.

All entries solve the same Phase 2 problem: predict the layerwise expected ReLU activations of a known width-1024, depth-16 random MLP under standard-normal input. The objective is the mean of per-network final-layer MSE multiplied by `max(0.1, FLOPs / 2**41)`. A lower raw error can still be a worse adjusted score if compute grows too much.
