## Method and contribution

Prune the nonlinear interface using |alpha| <= 2.5, repair omitted-row trace, and gate young sources from layer 5. Keeps inherited fitted tables; no new target-coefficient fit.

T160 exact P25a source: partial nonlinear interface |alpha|<=2.5 padded64, semilinear omitted-row trace repair, young-source prior gate>=.03 from layer5 padded64; keep inherited trace compensation, ranks320/192, Winograd/defaults, fitted tables and diagnostic arithmetic.

All entries solve the same Phase 2 problem: predict the layerwise expected ReLU activations of a known width-1024, depth-16 random MLP under standard-normal input. The objective is the mean of per-network final-layer MSE multiplied by `max(0.1, FLOPs / 2**41)`. A lower raw error can still be a worse adjusted score if compute grows too much.
