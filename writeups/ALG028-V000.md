## Method and contribution

Replaces the terminal harmonic-skew correction with a radial-law-corrected physical third-cumulant trace projection; retains the existing observer coefficients. A local mathematical adaptation, not a new fit.

Replace terminal harmonic skew correction with radial-law-corrected trace projection, reconstruct the fixed AS design on that carrier, retain parent closure and all coefficients.

All entries solve the same Phase 2 problem: predict the layerwise expected ReLU activations of a known width-1024, depth-16 random MLP under standard-normal input. The objective is the mean of per-network final-layer MSE multiplied by `max(0.1, FLOPs / 2**41)`. A lower raw error can still be a worse adjusted score if compute grows too much.
