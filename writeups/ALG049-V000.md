## Method and contribution

Starts from public V29 and adds the coupled native history basis, covariance-cup compensation for lost third-tensor trace, ranks320/192 and Winograd/shared-arena arithmetic. Inherits V29 calibration and core machinery; no new fit. The Winograd algorithm itself is established.

T158 P26 coupled native basis, principal lost-trace covariance cup, ranks320/192, shared-arena15-addition Winograd depth6/min16. Shipping change only encodes the four tested environment defaults, including the nested covariance-depth fallback. Keep diagnostic arithmetic and all other behavior.

All entries solve the same Phase 2 problem: predict the layerwise expected ReLU activations of a known width-1024, depth-16 random MLP under standard-normal input. The objective is the mean of per-network final-layer MSE multiplied by `max(0.1, FLOPs / 2**41)`. A lower raw error can still be a worse adjusted score if compute grows too much.
