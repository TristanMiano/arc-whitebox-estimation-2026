## Method and contribution

One Haar frame blended 0.25/0.75 with the bundled full-covariance closure. The project contribution is the concrete cost-aware composition; shrinkage and Gaussian closure are known.

replace four independent Phase2 Haar-Stiefel frame blocks by one block and blend every layer with fixed weights 0.25 frame and 0.75 bundled full-covariance closure while remaining below the score floor

fixed_frame_covariance_score_floor_shrinkage

All entries solve the same Phase 2 problem: predict the layerwise expected ReLU activations of a known width-1024, depth-16 random MLP under standard-normal input. The objective is the mean of per-network final-layer MSE multiplied by `max(0.1, FLOPs / 2**41)`. A lower raw error can still be a worse adjusted score if compute grows too much.
