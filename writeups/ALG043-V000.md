## Method and contribution

504aldo public V29 at commit18c17e2d7a9aeacd399cfc2c6b571e4e16dbfb45, numerical source byte-identical. Published terms, fitted tables, defaults and schedules unchanged. Only packaging, qualification and harness work were ours.

None relative to published V29: retain all source bytes, defaults, author-fit tables, shape riders, age/rank settings and counted multiplication schedules. No JSP/HGC combination or tuning.

All entries solve the same Phase 2 problem: predict the layerwise expected ReLU activations of a known width-1024, depth-16 random MLP under standard-normal input. The objective is the mean of per-network final-layer MSE multiplied by `max(0.1, FLOPs / 2**41)`. A lower raw error can still be a worse adjusted score if compute grows too much.
