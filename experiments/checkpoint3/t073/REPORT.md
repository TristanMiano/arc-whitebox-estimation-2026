## Frozen triage rule (fixed before ranking)

Each dimension is an integer from 0 to 4. The weighted score is

`S = (35*N + 30*E + 15*C + 20*R) / 4`, on a 0–100 scale.

- `N`, academic novelty: 0 known/duplicate; 1 crowded refinement; 2 plausible
  `N1` combination; 3 plausible `N2` mechanism; 4 isolated `N2` claim after a
  broad search.
- `E`, expected score effect from comparable evidence: 0 contraindicated or
  below 1%; 1 is 1–3%; 2 is 3–10%; 3 is 10–25%; 4 is above 25%.
- `C`, falsification/deployment economy: 0 infeasible or no bounded kill; 1
  expensive/near-budget; 2 bounded diagnostic before a cycle; 3 cheap kill and
  credible in-budget route; 4 static kill or ample-reserve direct route.
- `R`, robustness: 0 known uncontrolled bias/contradiction; 1 severe open
  bias/tail risk; 2 bounded but material risk; 3 exact target plus independent
  fit/fallback; 4 exact target with a deterministic safety identity and no
  known tail mechanism.

The selection floor is `S >= 55`. Rank by `S`, then `N`, then `R`. A hypothesis
also needs an exact or prospectively bias-bounded target, a credible operation
plan, a nonduplicate semantic delta, and a complete-estimator route. These
scores allocate tests; they are not M1/P1 evidence.

## `H073-A` — transported joint-kink arc-cosine control

**Closest public work.** Dead/on/kink classification and correlated threshold
crossings; analytic Gaussian/ReLU covariance; mid-network and ridge-ReLU
controls; cross-fitted/leave-half-out control fitting. The semantic delta is
the combination: use deterministic backward-transported near-threshold
directions to build **bivariate, exactly centered co-activation controls** for
the residual of an antithetic spherical rule. The ablation is joint product
features versus marginal features using the same transported directions and
matched work.

**Exact feature.** A closure-derived expected-gate linearization transports
layer-31 preactivation normals to constant input directions `a_i`. For a
selected pair `(i,k)`, use the antithetically even feature

`g_ik(x) = 0.5 * (relu(a_i^T x) relu(a_k^T x) + relu(-a_i^T x) relu(-a_k^T x))`.

On the incumbent radius-conditioned sphere `x = r_* u`, its mean is known from
the order-one arc-cosine kernel, multiplied by `r_*^2 / d`. Subtracting its
exact sphere mean therefore leaves the target identity unchanged even though
the feature is degree two. Candidate pair selection is deterministic from
closure gate uncertainty, final-output sensitivity, and predicted pair
correlation; it may not inspect reference targets.

**Falsifiable prediction.** Joint features explain residual variation missed
by marginal absolute-ridge controls because late ReLU switches are correlated.
On 12 fresh width-64/depth-32 synthetic networks with 512 antithetic pairs,
cross-fitted joint-feature residual trace variance must be `<0.90` of the
route-matched marginal-feature control, win at least `10/12` networks, and
have maximum network ratio `<1.25`. Failure of any condition rejects this
mechanism before a complete candidate is registered.

**Complete route if the kill passes.** At width 256, project the main points
onto the shared 256 transported normals once; select eight pair features per
final output; fit output-specific coefficients on independent folds; apply a
prospectively confidence-bounded fallback to zero correction; use a nested
dense sample count so candidate and marginal-feature control are compared by
the frozen adjusted score. The projected-normal matrix multiply and pair
products must fit inside the incumbent's measured/modelled reserve before F0.
The actual incumbent remains mandatory F2/F3 context.

**Risks.** The expected-gate normals may still be input-anchored enough to
decorrelate; feature selection may overfit; degree-two features may target
low-order content the carrier already integrates; per-output fitting can
create route tails. Independent folds, a marginal-feature comparator, and an
immediate M1 kill address these risks.

## `H073-B` — thresholded transport-ridge residual control

**Closest public work.** Proposed exact-mean sums of ReLU ridge functions,
StackMC/cross-fitting, and the observation that low-order input controls go
null under structured nets. The semantic delta is to place a frozen threshold
in an antithetically even, positively homogeneous ridge feature so its spectral
mass moves toward the kink/high-order residual that the carrier does not
already remove.

Use shared transported directions `a_i` and

`psi_i,t(x) = ||x|| relu(|a_i^T x|/||x|| - t ||a_i||/sqrt(d))`,

with prospectively fixed thresholds. This is degree-one homogeneous. Its
spherical/Gaussian mean is a one-dimensional beta integral and is embedded
with a certified constant. The nearest mechanism control is the same
directions, split, feature count, and operation plan at threshold `t=0`.

The cheap kill uses the same 12-network/512-pair fixture. It requires pooled
cross-fitted residual trace-variance ratio `<0.92`, at least `9/12` wins, and
maximum ratio `<1.25` against `t=0`. If it passes, T077 registers a complete
candidate and continues immediately through F0–F3. Bias risk is low because
the control mean is analytic; the main risks are selection and duplicating
carrier-resolved spectrum.

## Parked and closed hypotheses

`H073-C`, compressed suffix-K3, would use a shallow corrected K3/Hermite anchor
as a mid-network subtraction control rather than a predictor. Project evidence
supports shallow K3 and public work reports useful mid-network controls, but
the exact conceptual combination is already public, the center-bias problem is
known, and the operation plan is less favorable. It stays parked as a score
component, not an academic-priority candidate.

`H073-E` would intersect a forward angular-attractor direction with the
low-rank backward transport to select a carrier subframe. The forward/backward
connection may be academically interesting, but public subframe selection and
local global/lifted rotations have severe frozen tails or aggregate losses.
There is no current robust target-free selector. The slate closes this
operational form rather than spending another protected cycle on orientation.

`H073-D` is T072. Cross-fitting and a confidence cap could plausibly repair the
one T034 tail, but neither is a new estimator principle, the exact ridge-ReLU
control family is publicly proposed, and the best comparable full F2 evidence
suggests only a `1.11%` aggregate effect. T072 is therefore complete as a
documented no-run priority closure, not mislabeled as a scientific negative.
