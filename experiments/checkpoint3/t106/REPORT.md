# T106 — competitive-leverage research loop

## Outcome

The research loop is prospectively restructured. Beginning with FC007, every
new cycle starts with a default minimum of 60 active wall-clock minutes of
written theoretical hypothesis generation. The owner may override that
duration; the latest explicit direction controls, and the agent cannot shorten
it unilaterally. Each theory recurrence authorizes at most two candidate
decisions and at most 60 combined active minutes of default exploratory
execution before theory recurs. Under the current setting, theory therefore
occupies at least half of the default discretionary cycle budget.

No protected candidate result was opened, no algorithm identity or scientific
tier changed, and no submission succession occurred. `ALG018-V000` remains
the local and officially graded incumbent.

## What now receives priority

Each theory recurrence must publish equations, derivations, failure boundaries,
prior project rejection/positive-mechanism synthesis, dated public literature
and disclosed-contest-method synthesis, and at least three mechanism-distinct
hypotheses. Each hypothesis must quantify its lawful FLOPs, score multiplier,
expected raw MSE and adjusted score, uncertainty, and prior probability of
beating `ALG018-V000`.

The new 100-point ranking is intentionally dominated by competitive and
theoretical leverage:

| Axis | Points |
|---|---:|
| Competitive upside against the current Phase-2 incumbent | 40 |
| Theoretical support and failure-boundary quality | 25 |
| Robustness, breadth, tails, and operational behavior | 15 |
| Academic novelty | 10 |
| Information gained per unit test cost | 10 |

Novelty remains necessary for the contribution objective, but an idea without
a lawful compute-adjusted path to the incumbent cannot lead the score lane.

## Submission-first kill sequence

S0--S2 are expressly competition/submission screens, not rigorous scientific
evaluation. Their purpose is to decide whether a candidate is likely to beat
the current project/leaderboard incumbent well enough to succeed, package, or
submit.

Selected hypotheses now traverse:

1. S0 outcome-blind lawfulness, cost, and optimistic-score boundary;
2. S1 the cheapest lawful mechanism kill; and
3. S2 a direct frozen current-Phase-2 comparison on at least 12 route-matched
   networks against `ALG018-V000`.

A direct pooled adjusted ratio below one triggers the existing mandatory bold
succession rule. A ratio at least one closes the exact unchanged submission
branch immediately. Interesting M1/P1 or novelty evidence is preserved, but it
cannot keep the score branch alive after direct competitive negation.

Historical F0--F3 expansion becomes optional and decision-driven. Historical
P1 alone no longer authorizes full rigorous evaluation. Nor does an S2 win:
that win keeps the competitive branch alive and can lead through the existing
hard package gate to owner-authorized official submission before rigorous
science.

Full rigorous science is deferred through the candidate competition loop and
normally applies only to the final project best after the relevant batch or
recurrence, or to a successfully officially graded candidate within a near-top
leaderboard band frozen prospectively from the then-current public leaderboard.
The candidate must also remain lawful and mechanistically viable and pose a
question that changes final selection or an academic-contribution claim.

A noncompetitive academic exception remains possible, but it must name the
theorem, ablation, or contribution deliverable; explain why a cheaper test is
insufficient; and freeze its budget. The default cap is 30 active minutes and
24 networks. A larger exception needs a new owner decision and never revives
the submission branch.

## Why this corrects the observed failure mode

RC002 was scientifically coherent but competitively low-leverage:
`ALG017-V002` had already scored `1.505849` of then-incumbent `ALG015-V000`,
which was later surpassed by `ALG018-V000`. Historical P1 nevertheless
activated a 72-route rigorous cycle. The new policy prevents this path:
historical P1 is descriptive evidence, a direct current-shape win is required
to keep the score branch alive, and even a win does not automatically trigger
rigorous science before late selection.

T101's positive ALG017 mechanism evidence remains visible—R1 missed only B3
`1.003198`—but its P2 and submission branches are closed. No more unchanged
ALG017 compute is scheduled.
