`ALG014-V000` is the project's first **P1-promising** algorithm. On 32 fresh
E0004 networks, its pooled adjusted-score proxy was `0.54693281` of the frozen
equal-work iid spherical-antithetic removal control, a `45.31%` reduction. Both

already beaten prior project best `ALG013-V001` by `51.75%` on their directly
comparable F1 routes. E0004 does not contain a same-cohort predecessor run, so
this result confirms P1 but does not manufacture a new succession comparison.
It is a local native proxy—not an official grade, translated package, R1, P2,
or submission-readiness claim.

contest-specific realization was not found, but implementation specificity is

and [Geometrically Coupled Monte Carlo Sampling](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2018/hash/9dcb88e0137649590b755372b040afad-Abstract.html).

carries `0.321928` bits of surprise. This empirical success raises confidence
in collective negative dependence, not in T091's rejected leverage weights.

registration. After that repair, three exact Clang 17 attempts crashed while
parsing large inherited reference arrays, before any estimator executed. The
reproducers and logs are preserved. Removing two unused inherited caches from
the translation unit fixed the resource/layout path without changing arithmetic.

completed, and GCC 13 reproduced F3A/000 byte-for-byte. No owner action or clean
streak was required.
