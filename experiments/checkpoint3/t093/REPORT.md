## H090-C — selected exact correction

`A(R0)` be a fixed cheap subframe output already contained in that carrier.
For independent Haar orientations `R1,...,Rk`, a pilot-frozen diagonal response

```text
Z = Q(R0) - B [A(R0) - mean_j A(Rj)].
```

exactly zero expectation. T094 must validate that identity, independence,

may freeze a bounded subframe/center allocation and diagonal response, but no
