## Main theoretical results

### 1. LPITF: full-chain independent-truth fusion

The mean-transport operator is the full product of
`diag(Phi(alpha_l)) W_l^T` factors.  A stable two-sided randomized range
finder constructs its top left/output space without explicitly materializing
that product and without the invalid per-column normalization found during
the adversarial audit.  Ranks `{8,16,32}`, oversampling four, clean and
retained source graphs, scalar shrink, and a strictly qualified leading/rest
block rule are prospectively fixed.

The central test is not whether the space has large singular values.  Two
independent truth-observation sources estimate the cross-product between the
proposed closure correction and the latent closure error on held whole
networks.  Full-chain, random-rank, and terminal-local projectors receive the
same rank/cost grammar.  This directly addresses prior evidence that a full
transport product is necessary while top-operator energy alone is not enough.

### 2. DALT: exact noisy-audit risk and better production geometry

For a zero-bias ReLU MLP, positive homogeneity makes a Haar mean-radius audit
an unbiased observation of the Gaussian input mean.  Two genuinely child-
separated full bases `A/B` provide development replicates.  Their cross and
difference products estimate, without true targets, the incumbent's full risk,
the risk captured in a response subspace, and audit noise.

The production estimator need not average two smaller half-bases.  T113
derived a same-cost alternative: fit on one full-basis prefix and validate the
whole nonlinear candidate on the other full replicate.  T115 will compare
this **single-full** geometry against equal dual halves, rather than assuming
either is universally better.  A guarded 2,048/4,096 independent-basis branch
opens only when the frozen continuous allocation calculation says the
incumbent's captured risk can pay for it.

### 3. One exact selector for every audit carrier

If candidate `C_A` is fit using audit A and evaluated with independent audit
B, the symmetric held raw-risk difference is unbiased.  Combining that raw
difference `D` with the independent full parent-risk estimate `T` yields

```text
DeltaAdjusted = q_C (T + D) - q_B T.
```

This corrects a subtle but material draft defect: separately weighting noisy
candidate and parent losses by different score multipliers introduces audit-
noise bias.  The corrected tuple `(T,D,q_C,q_B)` is bootstrapped intact.  The
same identity lets T115 select DALT, a signed nine-endpoint NASES stack, or a
cheap NAAR incumbent reblend before loading protected means.

### 4. Flexible submission gate retained

The final competition screen requires an expected adjusted score below the
incumbent and whole-network bootstrap `P(win) > .50`.  Confidence intervals,
the former strict `.95 * incumbent` margin, and control/mechanism separation
remain visible diagnostics but are not submission bans.  This matches the
owner's “likely to succeed” direction and preserves promising evidence that a
scientific-confidence gate would otherwise hide.  Rigorous scientific
adjudication remains deferred until a final best or near-frontier result.

## Rejections, implementation-risk repairs, and boundaries

- A pooled full-matrix DALT-W shrink was rejected before code because
  per-network response bases have arbitrary signs and rotations; scalar or
  separated blocks remain valid.
- Four-way audit splitting was rejected because it worsens production-frame
  variance at fixed total rows.
- Characteristic-function smoothing was rejected because the ReLU kink-bias
  bound requires an impractically high cutoff before meaningful variance
  benefit.
- A/B audit sources must be separated from each other and from incumbent
  streams.  Shared noise creates positive covariance that inflates apparent
  signal and understates audit noise.
- DALT adaptation and the large-basis branch are disabled, without stalling
  fixed-cost carriers, when the full-risk audit is semantically valid but too
  noisy for absolute calibration.
- Completed unfavorable statistical outputs are evidence and are not rerun
  for a better seed.  Operational or implementation failures are diagnosed
  and retried; work pauses only when bounded self-repair makes owner action
  reasonably necessary.

The exact disposition of every pass/fail pattern is in the theory ledger's
final human-readable outcome matrix.  A project-created random/local control
is succession-eligible if it is the best lawful N1 estimator; the word
“control” cannot conceal a better algorithm.  Public A716 alone is N0 and does
not satisfy the owner's at-least-somewhat-novel submission standard.
