# T156 — Empirical feedback during theory work

The research protocol now allows numerous small experiments throughout a
recurrence: derive, run a probe, interpret its result and revise the derivation.
An authorized recurrence includes partial prototypes, toy counterexamples,
state traces, small development comparisons and targeted parameter/rank
sensitivities. A complete candidate is unnecessary before testing a hypothesis.

There is no fixed experiment-count limit, compulsory submission-sized panel or
90% confidence requirement for exploration. Keep a short scratchpad entry per
probe or batch identifying the question, executed version, inputs and comparator;
retain the outputs and note what the result changed. A full algorithm registry
entry is needed for the selected submission candidate, not every exploratory
variant. Use the smallest informative runs and track their cumulative cost.

The added feedback should help pursue credible larger gains, including ideas
with uncertain prospects. Targeted parameter experiments are permitted; they
do not replace the standing preference for developing a promising larger
opportunity when an easy small improvement appears.
