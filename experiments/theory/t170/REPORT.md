## What the ambitious work taught us

**Compression loses more than its direct mean observation.** The tensor
projection work derives exact diagonal/trace completion, a residual Frobenius
metric, a mean-sensitive Gaussian query metric, and an explicit carrier
preserving the mean-axis cubic contraction. Generated identities pass. Yet
retaining 512 source columns still roughly doubles actual raw error after these
repairs. Retaining 768 can save compute with a smaller accuracy loss.

Fitting retained weights to diagonal or a few source queries makes those chosen
observables better while worsening independent queries. An exact single-source
Gram analysis finds little ideal reweighting capacity in the retained span.
Thus another weight fit in that same span has weak prospects.

Saved native trajectories and one targeted state capture give a clearer failure
mechanism. On the inspected F0compression case, variance and cubic perturbations
dominate terminal error; changes in the fourth scalar and neural response are
small. These are correlated differential components, not causal percentages.
A Gaussian identity explains why: even when the immediate cubic mean correction
vanishes at zero mean, the mixed-third covariance channel has squared error
proportional to the omitted tensor's Frobenius energy. It can therefore harm
the following layer. A repair aimed only at immediate means misses that channel.

Removing the attributed variance component at zero cost still leaves1.11949×
adjusted error on F0. Removing both variance and cubic components gives.870898×,
but this uses unavailable error information and is not a realizable estimator.
It narrows the next question: a useful compact carrier must address both effects.
Some one-leg tensor matrices have promising spectral decay, but we have not
built or priced a constrained carrier, and those snapshots precede the actual
pruning location. Joint compression across births is also untested.

**Combining modest compression with covariance correction bought too little.**
P50's eight-network adjusted ratio .966588 slightly improves on .969975, but
raw error rises1.93% and residual time reaches.429424s. P52 shares Gram work,
preserving two checked predictions byte-for-byte; residual still reaches
.420374s. The additional roughly.34percentage-point adjusted gain does not
justify another repair campaign now. The simpler uncombined source leads.

**More flexible fits did not automatically help.** A 22-field endpoint-conditioned
neural covariance closure finished .969898 on its held fold, worse than the
.963187 constant initialization. A joint mean/covariance head and two newly
derived covariance-memory inputs also failed their fixed recipes. This rejects
those models and objectives, not all learned dynamics. An explicit pair
invariant may convey information that endpoint-average coefficients cannot;
its incremental held-network capacity remains unmeasured.

**Higher scalar order alone remains weak.** Single-site, Gaussian-tree and loop
fifth/sixth observers produced negligible or inconsistent saved-trajectory
response gains. A new
cross-birth third-to-fifth insertion identity was derived and checked. Its cheap
first weight-chaos observer changed two saved-trajectory readout errors by only .0025%/.0476%.
Covariance attachments, inherited fifth state and higher weight chaos remain
untested; no full fifth-order algorithm is claimed to have failed.

## Research character, literature and remaining choices

This was a combination of deduction and empirical fitting. Algebra determined
the covariance directions, tensor projections, adjoints and failure identities.
Supervised ridge selected their practical defect coefficients; several small
neural alternatives were tested and lost. The nominee is therefore a **learned
adaptation of a known cumulant framework**, not a parameter-free new theory or
a copy of a published complete estimator. No academic-priority claim is made.

The [primary-source notes](LITERATURE.md) cover operator corrections, moment
closure, tensor compression, goal-oriented approximation and public challenge
code. No directly reusable stronger complete estimator was identified in this
focused search. An [October4 participant report](https://discourse.aicrowd.com/t/everything-we-tried-a-factorized-k-3-cumulant-propagation-estimator-at-0-25-x-b-where-its-flops-go-and-25-measured-dead-ends-team-504aldo-rank-10/18218/6)
supplies a narrow deeper-Winograd-leaf lead. Our implementation already takes
most of that depth improvement and uses a different kernel; the remaining
L7/min8 change is untested and is recorded as a small reserve.

The [selection document](CANDIDATE_SELECTION.md) lists all untested candidates,
subjective success/gain ranges, remaining work and scoped exhaustion estimates.
The more promising larger nearby question is a **specific pair-conditioned
covariance defect**; its current forecast is 15--25% for 5--15% conditional gain
after 4--8 hours. More distant distributional recurrences retain larger upside
but lack a concrete affordable state. These are research options, not a queue
of automatic implementation tasks.
