## The most useful new derivations

A fourth residual produces new third-order structure after ReLU. I derived
its finite factor form, harmonic-core subtraction and repeated-index removal,
and checked them against dense tensors. The all-distinct new cubic tensor has
zero ordinary trace at birth, but generally acquires trace after the next
weight map. A birth-trace-only correction therefore cannot recover it.

That identifies a genuine incompleteness in our fourth-history prototypes.
It does **not** establish that filling it will improve score. A combined-source
fixed-state diagnostic has a substantial response, but its terminal correction
barely aligns with the observed error and makes that case46.7%worse at full
scale. A useful successor must update covariance, cubic birth and regenerated
core consistently and cheaply. It is still unbuilt.

The rank-one compression diagnostic found a related distinction: cubic
Gaussian-ray error depends on both tensor norm and its trace. An unconstrained
fit can reduce tensor norm while worsening the observable. Harmonic atoms
preserve trace exactly, but their remaining approximation error and transported
metric cost must still be addressed. [Derivations](SCRATCHPAD.md), especially
sections35–40; [probe record](PROBES.md).
