## Why the selected method works better

A third tensor can lose its trace even when the retained basis contains that
trace direction: projecting the two contracted tensor legs changes the trace
itself. The new residual carries the lost vector after compression. It lets us
reduce native ranks while retaining enough useful information to beat the
incumbent's adjusted score. Its covariance-cup reset is deliberately inexpensive.
Making that transport more exact, or including every thin-source trace term,
improves raw accuracy slightly but costs more than it saves.

On the matched six-network panel, the physical trace-compensated lower-rank
method improves adjusted score2.96%. The new arithmetic then improves that
method by another4.84%, giving7.65% combined. The eight-network aggregate is
7.45%. This is mostly an efficiency improvement; it is not evidence that the
approximate moment state is now more accurate than the incumbent.

The source combines existing native V29 machinery, the coupled metric derived
in T153, a new repository adaptation for discarded trace, and classical
Winograd identities. No claim of academic novelty is needed for the submission
case. The tested source, exact configuration and provenance are preserved.

## Where progress was disappointing or incomplete

The broader search did **not** produce a convincing large upgrade in moment
accuracy. Several theoretically richer constructions either changed the wrong
response, conflicted with a valid joint law, or needed more work than their
small accuracy gain could repay. We have concrete negative evidence for the
specified variants, but no proof that non-Gaussian joint states, conditional
closures or complete fourth history are exhausted.

A useful new building block is the persistent harmonic fourth-star contraction,
which can reuse native A/P transports cheaply. Later chain diagnostics found a structural zero-diagonal repair and an affordable source range; the trace projection and complete state response still need a finite implementation. Its tested mean-only correction
is not a win. Adding chain terms and the missing covariance/fourth feedback is
an open design with a real cost problem, not an already specified successor.
Likewise, late gate packing has a measured primitive, but its likely overall
saving is modest and the complete layout/state integration remains unbuilt.

The most valuable empirical change was testing the complete candidate early
and expanding promising results before nomination. That replaced a15–30%
geometry-led forecast with an integrated candidate around90%. The official
return from this process remains pending, so one round does not establish a
long-run research-efficiency trend or a new mandatory protocol.
