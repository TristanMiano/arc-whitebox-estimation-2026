# Academic contribution and score submissions

**Current scope:** T123's submission-focused workflow. Earlier novelty audits
and dated prior-art discussions are preserved in the
[pre-T123 snapshot](../experiments/protocol/t123/before/docs/ACADEMIC_NOVELTY_AND_SUBMISSION_POLICY.md).

The project seeks both better official scores and a defensible algorithmic
contribution. A known method or useful combination is eligible for the score
track. Novelty and scientific tiers are not submission prerequisites.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| N0-KNOWN-BASELINE | Known method or implementation refinement; useful for score without an invention claim |
| N1-NOVEL-COMBINATION | A non-obvious combination whose structural contribution can be explained |
| N2-NOVEL-MECHANISM | A new estimator principle or mechanism with a falsifiable prediction |
| NU-UNCERTAIN | Prior-art boundary is unresolved; avoid an unsupported novelty claim |

Before a materially new candidate, search the algorithm ledger for duplicates
and relevant failures, record the exact delta and a brief provenance/novelty
assessment. Reuse existing audits for inherited mechanisms. A focused prior-art
check belongs in the theory work when needed; a full literature census or a
quota of novelty packets is not required before every submission.

A theory recurrence retains the owner's minimum of 60 active written-math
minutes and ends in a value-ranked slate of zero to two candidate decisions.
Its equations, cost model, failure boundaries and cheapest decisive test
matter more than a fixed number of ideas or a weighted novelty rubric. Follow
[the current loop](COMPETITIVE_RESEARCH_LOOP.md).

A contribution write-up needs an explicit new claim, closest disclosed prior
art, derivation, supporting evidence, limitations and transparent provenance.
Choose its experiments as a separately scoped scientific task. A higher
leaderboard score alone does not establish the proposed mechanism; an ablation
is not mandatory before a useful score submission. Full science is never
started automatically because a candidate is novel or grades well.

Final selection/designation and submission dates follow the current official
rules. Ordinary uploads neither consume a final designation decision nor
imply that one was made. Keep the best reliable package while exploring.
