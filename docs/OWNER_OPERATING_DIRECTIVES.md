## Current intent

The objective is better official ARC submissions in the shortest useful time.
The owner wants the project to stop drifting into expensive experiments and
unnecessary scientific rigor. Roughly 90% confidence that a lawful submission
will improve the score is enough to try it. The official grader's battery is
part of the feedback loop, and submissions are relatively cheap.

The owner accepted conditional task ordering and ranking by expected official
improvement per remaining hour. A reserved candidate is not compulsory. Return
to theory early when the remaining successor is unpromising or poor value.

The detailed prospective workflow is [COMPETITIVE_RESEARCH_LOOP.md](COMPETITIVE_RESEARCH_LOOP.md),
mirrored in [state/competitive_research_policy.json](../state/competitive_research_policy.json).
This replaces earlier universal funnel instructions for new submission work.
Historical scientific freezes and results remain unchanged.

## Cadence, theory and time

- Work on one requested TODO task, save its decision and stop, unless the owner
  explicitly authorizes a longer cadence. Follow the active queue rather than
  an unchecked parked/historical entry.
- Under the T156 amendment, a theory recurrence requires at least **120 active
  research minutes**, including **60 minutes of written mathematics**, possibly
  longer. Only the owner can shorten the applicable floor. Small-prototype
  implementation and empirical interpretation count as research, but not
  automatically as mathematics; passive waiting and packaging do not count.
  For **T122 specifically**, the owner raised the minimum to **two hours**;
  [its result](../experiments/theory/t122/REPORT.md) records the completed
  120-active-minute recurrence. For **T126 specifically**, the owner also
  requested at least **two active hours**; [its result](../experiments/theory/t126/REPORT.md)
  records **120.32 active minutes** completed. These task-specific overrides do not
  change the default for future tasks. T156's later standing amendment does.
- Nominate zero to two final candidates from a recurrence; re-rank after
  evidence. This does not limit exploratory probes or variants. Do not execute
  the second candidate by habit. Small decisive tests precede large work.
- Record start, a realistic runtime estimate, end, actual duration and estimate
  error. Report a rough analysis/implementation/execution-wait allocation.
  Time ceilings are not time to fill. Count all remaining time when ranking
  work, including packaging, repairs and official grading.

## Submission authority and waiting

An explicit request to execute a submission task authorizes its preparation,
qualification, upload and bounded monitoring. A request to make a bounded
attempt to submit a named variant has the same scope. Do not ask again merely
because upload follows routine preparation. Inserting an upload task alone
supplies no external-upload authority; prepare it fully before asking if such
an authorization is actually missing. Do not infer final designation.

The owner's T161 amendment (2026-09-20UTC) sets **60 minutes per submission
try as the standing default**, measured from acceptance and including an
already active try. Retain at most one unchanged-package retry
for a generic evaluator failure or timeout. Preserve every submission ID and
result. Diagnose a concrete candidate defect separately; do not repeatedly
resubmit a valid but disappointing score.

## Evidence, repair and reporting

Keep required rules, numerical-accounting, output, resource and archive checks.
Do not add scientific tiers, arbitrary effect thresholds, multi-block studies,
clean-run streaks or multi-platform campaigns to a sufficient submission
screen. Reuse relevant evidence and the working environment. WSL/Linux is
already qualified for the inherited submission route; repeatedly demonstrating
the earlier Windows residual failure is unnecessary.

Preserve failures, inspect the smallest useful diagnostic and make bounded
in-scope repairs. Routine repairs and clearly identified production realizations
within the authorized task do not require another permission pause. Ask only
when a remaining action actually needs owner authority or intervention, and
state that action, evidence and reason. Do not prescribe a blind reboot.

Use one concise task result. State what was learned, the actual blocker if any,
the strongest favorable evidence, and the next useful action. Distinguish
competitive/scientific negatives from operational failures, incomplete evidence
and discretionary parking. Report only applicable gates; do not enumerate an
unrun scientific ladder for every small task. Announce a supported submission
candidate and a better official grade in bold. Keep scientific tiers, novelty,
local scores, projections and official results separate.

## Next recurrence: research floor and larger gains — 2026-09-06, after T135

The owner accepted a combined literature/theory task and the baseline allocation
of20 minutes literature,80 minutes active theory, and20 minutes candidate
selection/handoff, but made the two-hour total a **floor, not a ceiling**.
The proposed150-minute hard ceiling is withdrawn. Plan at least120 minutes of
useful task work, including at least80 active theory minutes; keep administration
lean and put spare or additional time into research. Idle waiting does not count.
Continue beyond two hours while a concrete line of reasoning warrants it; use
elapsed time as a reassessment point rather than an automatic stopping rule.

If an inexpensive candidate becomes apparent alongside a plausible route to a
substantially larger score reduction, record the cheap candidate and prioritize
the more ambitious route. Finding something ready to test does not itself end
the recurrence. Balance plausible gain, probability of success, remaining work,
and the information a test would provide. Preserve inexpensive candidates as
fallbacks; do not impose a minimum submission gain or dismiss small improvements.
Small wins are uncertain too. Existing lean submission checks remain unchanged.

## Interleave small experiments with derivations — 2026-09-11, T156

The owner requested permission for “numerous, small experiments” during
derivations, to get empirical feedback while developing hypotheses, and asked
that time floors increase accordingly. This applies to future recurrences,
including the proposed T153. It does not start that recurrence in this turn.

An authorized recurrence includes repeated exploratory runs, partial
prototypes, small development comparisons and targeted parameter/rank
sensitivities. No separate permission, complete algorithm or high win
probability is needed for each probe. Use a short reproducible scratchpad/batch
record, declare exploratory data, preserve outcomes and revise the mathematics
as feedback arrives. There is no fixed experiment-count limit. Manage total
cost and learning; avoid drifting into an expensive campaign through many
individually small runs. Continue to give credible larger improvements room.

To implement the requested increase, the assistant set the default to120 active
research minutes with60 written mathematics preserved. T153's proposed total
increases from180 to240:120 derivation,30 literature,30 selection and60 added
for empirical work interleaved throughout. The added allowance can support
further substantive derivation when more probes would teach little; total
and existing component floors remain. Count active time once, with waiting
reported separately. These exact allocations are implementation choices under
the owner's request, not numbers the owner explicitly specified.

Exploratory data may be reused adaptively with that role recorded. Existing
freezes and the final blind block remain protected; selected candidates still
use the lean submission decision and required production checks. No new
scientific gate, automatic upload or agent authorization is added.
